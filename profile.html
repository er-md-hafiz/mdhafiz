A little about me

Engineering products people rely on.

I’m Md Hafiz, a Senior Android Developer with 8.5+ years of experience building and maintaining production Android applications across news, healthcare and automotive domains.

I value clean architecture, pragmatic problem-solving, thoughtful collaboration and mentoring — with a focus on shipping maintainable software that performs reliably in the real world.
